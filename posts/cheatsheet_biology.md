# Biology Cheatsheet

## Cell Structure
- **Nucleus**: Contains DNA, controls cell activities
- **Mitochondria**: Powerhouse of the cell, produces ATP
- **Endoplasmic Reticulum**: Protein and lipid synthesis
- **Golgi Apparatus**: Packages and ships proteins
- **Lysosomes**: Break down waste materials

## Photosynthesis
- Process: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
- Occurs in chloroplasts
- Light-dependent and light-independent reactions

## Cellular Respiration
- Process: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ATP
- Occurs in mitochondria
- Glycolysis, Krebs Cycle, Electron Transport Chain

## DNA and Genetics
- DNA: Deoxyribonucleic acid - stores genetic information
- RNA: Ribonucleic acid - transfers genetic information
- Base pairs: A-T, G-C
- Genes are segments of DNA that code for proteins

## Evolution
- Natural Selection: Survival of the fittest
- Adaptation: Traits that increase survival
- Species: Organisms that can reproduce and have fertile offspring
