# Chemistry Cheatsheet

## Periodic Table Basics
- Elements are organized by atomic number
- Organized in rows (periods) and columns (groups)
- Period indicates number of electron shells
- Group indicates valence electrons

## Atomic Structure
- Protons: Positively charged particles in nucleus
- Neutrons: Neutral particles in nucleus
- Electrons: Negatively charged particles orbiting nucleus

## Chemical Bonds
- **Covalent Bond**: Sharing of electrons between atoms
- **Ionic Bond**: Transfer of electrons from one atom to another
- **Metallic Bond**: Electrons flow freely among metal atoms

## States of Matter
- **Solid**: Definite shape and volume
- **Liquid**: Definite volume, takes shape of container
- **Gas**: No definite shape or volume
- **Plasma**: Ionized gas

## pH Scale
- pH < 7: Acidic
- pH = 7: Neutral
- pH > 7: Basic/Alkaline
