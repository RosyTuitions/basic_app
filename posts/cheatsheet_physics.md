# Physics Cheatsheet

## Mechanics
- **Newton's First Law**: Object at rest stays at rest unless acted upon by force
- **Newton's Second Law**: F = ma
- **Newton's Third Law**: For every action, there is equal and opposite reaction

## Motion
- **Velocity**: Rate of change of position (v = d/t)
- **Acceleration**: Rate of change of velocity (a = Δv/Δt)
- **Displacement**: Change in position

## Energy
- **Kinetic Energy**: KE = ½mv²
- **Potential Energy**: PE = mgh
- **Conservation of Energy**: Total energy remains constant

## Waves
- **Wavelength**: Distance between consecutive crests
- **Frequency**: Number of waves per unit time
- **Wave Speed**: v = f × λ
- **Types**: Mechanical and electromagnetic waves

## Electricity and Magnetism
- **Voltage**: Electric potential difference (V = W/Q)
- **Current**: Flow of charge (I = Q/t)
- **Resistance**: Opposition to current flow (R = V/I)
- **Ohm's Law**: V = IR
- **Magnetic Field**: Region of space where magnetic force acts

## Light
- **Speed of light**: c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
- **Reflection**: Light bounces off surface
- **Refraction**: Light bends when entering different medium
- **Diffraction**: Light bends around obstacles
